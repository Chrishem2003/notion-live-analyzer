# Phase 136 — Authorization History Registry Review Lifecycle

Phase 136 converts reconciled Phase 134 human-review evidence into deterministic lifecycle states: OPEN, ACKNOWLEDGED, DEFERRED, or ESCALATED.

Review outcomes map to lifecycle states while preserving the exact monitor and review fingerprints. Lifecycle bundles require a Phase 135 RECONCILED result.

The lifecycle is human-governed, read-only, and execution-closed. It cannot repair, mutate, enforce, dispatch, or establish environmental or regulatory truth. NEMA-AGORA remains an independent student-led prototype.
