# Phase 18 - Shadow Monitoring & Evidence Observatory

Phase 18 adds an operational evidence layer above the Phase 17 controlled-shadow ledger.

It measures execution volume, failures, latency, unique-case coverage, human-review contract compliance, advisory safety-contract compliance, and model identity consistency.

It does not convert shadow outputs into environmental truth, regulatory status, enforcement decisions, emergency dispatch, or model accuracy.

Monitoring states:
- NOT_ENOUGH_DATA
- HEALTHY_WITHIN_SHADOW_POLICY
- WATCH
- CONTROL_REQUIRED

Phases 13-16 establish pre-admission evidence. Phases 17-18 establish controlled operational shadow evidence.
