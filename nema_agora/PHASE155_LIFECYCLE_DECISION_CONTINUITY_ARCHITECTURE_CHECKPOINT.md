# Phase 155 — Lifecycle Decision Continuity and Architecture Checkpoint

Phase 155 establishes continuity evidence for Phase 153 lifecycle authorization decisions. Snapshots are sequential, predecessor-bound, deterministic, and validated without repair or execution.

## Architecture checkpoint
The project should not continue generating mechanically repetitive governance layers solely because a numeric phase is available. After this checkpoint, the planner must compare proposed work against material product value. Reusable governance primitives should be preferred over another near-identical monitor/review/lifecycle chain.

Priority should shift toward end-to-end environmental evidence workflows, provenance, spatial intelligence, reviewer experience, evaluation, security, observability, reporting, reproducibility, and deployment readiness.

## Safety
Continuity is evidence of record consistency only. It does not establish environmental truth, regulatory status, NEMA authorization, production approval, enforcement authority, or emergency response.
