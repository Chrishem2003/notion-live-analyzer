# Phase 160 — Product Architecture Checkpoint

Phase 160 transitions NEMA-AGORA from registry-specific governance proliferation to reusable product capabilities.

## Product spine
Observation -> Evidence -> Quality -> Provenance -> Advisory Intelligence -> Human Review -> Export/Reporting -> Evaluation

The evidence_case module is the first reusable product primitive on that spine. It packages evidence references, deterministic observation provenance, advisory findings, human review, and a closed export gate.

## Boundaries
- Independent student-led prototype; no NEMA endorsement or official integration.
- No fabricated environmental observations, official boundaries, or regulatory data.
- AI findings remain advisory.
- Human review is required before export.
- Export is packaging only, never official submission.
- No autonomous enforcement, emergency dispatch, or regulatory determination.
- Validation is deterministic and read-only; no automatic repair.

## Build rule
Do not add another narrowly renamed registry/review/reconciliation layer unless it closes a demonstrated product or integrity gap. Prefer reusable primitives, end-to-end workflows, reviewer UX, provenance, evaluation, reporting, observability, security, accessibility, and deployment readiness.

## Acceptance target
Trace one case from source observation through evidence quality and advisory analysis to human review and export, with every transition attributable, reproducible, and testable.
