# Phase 156 — Lifecycle Decision Continuity Registry

Phase 156 persists Phase 155 continuity snapshots in SQLite with unique sequence/fingerprint constraints and database triggers that reject UPDATE and DELETE operations.

The registry is an evidence-retention mechanism only. It does not repair records, execute decisions, authorize environmental action, establish regulatory status, or imply NEMA integration.

The next planned step is a registry health monitor followed by human review and reconciliation only where those layers add material operational value.
