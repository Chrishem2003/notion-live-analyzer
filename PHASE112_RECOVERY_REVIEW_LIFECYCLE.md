# Phase 112 — Recovery Review Lifecycle & Decision Governance
Phase 112 converts reconciled human recovery-review evidence into a deterministic lifecycle snapshot. It maps human outcomes to lifecycle states, validates evaluation timestamps and fingerprints, and explicitly records that recovery is not automatically performed.

Lifecycle states: ACKNOWLEDGED, DEFERRED, ESCALATED, APPROVED_NO_ACTION.

A lifecycle snapshot requires a reconciled review scope. It is evidence of human governance, not an execution command. Environmental, regulatory, enforcement and emergency conclusions remain unavailable.

Completion gate: focused Phase 112 tests, compilation and Streamlit smoke checks must visibly pass before CI-green is claimed.
