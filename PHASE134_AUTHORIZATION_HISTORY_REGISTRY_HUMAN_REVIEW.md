# Phase 134 — Authorization History Registry Human Review

Phase 134 introduces the human-governed review boundary for Phase 133 integrity-monitoring evidence.

## Outcomes
- `ACKNOWLEDGED` — only when the registry is healthy.
- `REVIEW_AUTHORIZATION_HISTORY_REGISTRY` — for no-history or control-required states.
- `PRESERVE_AND_ESCALATE` — only for control-required evidence.
- `ESCALATED` — human escalation for no-history or control-required evidence.

Every review binds to the exact Phase 133 monitor fingerprint. Reviews are advisory governance evidence; they do not execute recovery, modify registries, enforce regulations, trigger emergency response, or establish environmental/regulatory truth.

NEMA-AGORA remains an independent student-led prototype and does not imply NEMA endorsement or authorization.
