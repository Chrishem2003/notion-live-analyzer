# Phase 121 — Retention Authorization Decision

Phase 121 establishes an explicit human authorization boundary after Phase 120 lifecycle evaluation.

Decisions:
- AUTHORIZE_RETENTION_REVIEW — for DEFERRED lifecycle state.
- AUTHORIZE_PRESERVATION — for ACKNOWLEDGED or DEFERRED evidence.
- AUTHORIZE_ESCALATION — for ESCALATED lifecycle state.

Every decision is bound to monitor, review, and lifecycle fingerprints.

Safety boundary: human authorization is governance evidence only. Execution permitted and execution performed remain false, and the execution gate remains closed.

No retention repair, deletion, enforcement, emergency dispatch, official NEMA integration, or regulatory/environmental conclusion is performed.
