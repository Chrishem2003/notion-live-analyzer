# Phase 27 — Deployment & Demonstration Hardening

## Objective
Make NEMA-AGORA easy to demonstrate without confusing synthetic evidence with
real environmental observations.

## Controls
- Deterministic demo dataset with a reproducible fingerprint.
- Explicit synthetic-data and non-persistence flags.
- No personal data, precise user location, official integrations, emergency
  workflow, enforcement action, or autonomous decisions.
- Demonstration artifacts remain separate from pilot evidence.
- Demo data is for training, interface walkthroughs, and controlled testing.

## Award/demo principle
A strong demonstration should show the complete chain:
observation → quality → advisory intelligence → human review → evidence →
reproducibility, while clearly identifying what is synthetic.

## Boundary
This phase improves demonstration and deployment readiness. It does not
establish environmental impact, environmental truth, NEMA authorization,
regulatory approval, or production suitability.
