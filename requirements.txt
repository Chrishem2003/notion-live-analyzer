### Step 4: Handle Local vs. Cloud Backend URLs
If your FastAPI backend is running locally, your public Streamlit app won't be able to reach `[http://127.0.0.1:8000](http://127.0.0.1:8000)`. 
* **For Local Testing:** Run both services locally:
  * Terminal 1: `uvicorn backend_advanced:app --reload`[cite: 2]
  * Terminal 2: `streamlit run frontend_advanced.py`[cite: 2]
* **For Cloud Deployment:** Deploy your FastAPI backend to a host like Render, Railway, or Hugging Face Spaces, and update your `BACKEND_URL` variable in your Streamlit script to point to your live backend endpoint instead of `127.0.0.1`.
