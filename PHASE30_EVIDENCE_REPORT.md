# Phase 30 — Evidence Provenance Graph & Research Report

Phase 30 connects recorded provenance with the Phase 29 claim/evidence/limitation matrix. It creates a deterministic evidence graph and a structured research report artifact.

## Graph rules

- Provenance records become lineage nodes.
- Recorded parent IDs become lineage edges.
- Claim source IDs become support edges.
- Missing parents are explicit validation failures.
- Claims with no linked source are explicit orphan claims.
- Report identity is derived from the synthesis fingerprint and graph fingerprint.

## Boundaries

This is an engineering/research evidence artifact. It does not establish environmental truth or impact, regulatory status, NEMA authorization, production approval, enforcement, emergency response, or autonomous authority.

## Award-grade value

The report demonstrates that important claims are traceable to evidence and that broken lineage is surfaced instead of silently hidden. It provides a machine-readable foundation for a future human-reviewed presentation/report export.
