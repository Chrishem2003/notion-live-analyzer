# Phase 33 — Evidence Integrity & Audit Hardening

Phase 33 adds integrity checks across provenance records, evidence graphs, report claims and publication decisions.

Checks include duplicate provenance IDs, duplicate event identities, missing parents, duplicate graph node IDs, broken edges, graph fingerprint mismatch, graph identity mismatch, report-to-graph binding mismatch, duplicate/missing claim IDs, unresolved claim sources, and publication-decision/report binding mismatch.

A valid result means the supplied artifacts are internally consistent under this policy. It is not cryptographic proof of the truth of source observations, and it does not establish environmental impact, NEMA authorization, regulatory status, production approval, enforcement, or emergency response authority.
