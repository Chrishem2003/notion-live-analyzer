# Phase 31 — Research & Award Dossier

Phase 31 turns the evidence synthesis and provenance graph into a human-reviewed dossier structure.

## Sections

1. Executive Summary
2. Technical Contribution
3. Evidence & Results
4. Governance & Safety
5. Limitations & Next Steps

The dossier never invents missing measurements. Claims remain linked to evidence references and limitations. A dossier is `READY_FOR_HUMAN_REVIEW` only when claims are linked and the lineage graph has no orphan claims or missing parents; otherwise it is `CONTROL_REQUIRED`.

This is research/engineering documentation only. It does not establish environmental truth, environmental impact, NEMA authorization, regulatory status, production approval, enforcement, emergency response, or autonomous authority.
