# Phase 29 — Evidence Synthesis

## Purpose

Phase 29 converts evidence produced by earlier NEMA-AGORA laboratories into a
single auditable research narrative using a strict **claim → evidence →
limitation** structure.

## Evidence domains

1. ENGINEERING — tested controls and engineering evidence.
2. WORKFLOW_OUTCOMES — paired baseline/assisted workflow observations.
3. HUMAN_GOVERNANCE — human review, lifecycle and controlled-evaluation evidence.
4. REPRODUCIBILITY — provenance, dataset and software identity evidence.
5. ACCESSIBILITY — aggregate participation and accessibility observations.
6. FIELD_EVALUATION — controlled scenario execution through the real pipeline.

## Claim discipline

Every generated claim records:

- claim identifier;
- evidence domain and evidence type;
- source identifiers;
- sample size;
- limitations;
- support status.

A claim without source identifiers is labelled **EVIDENCE_THIN**. Every claim
must retain at least one limitation. The synthesis fingerprint is derived from
the canonical domain/claim/limitation payload, so identical inputs produce the
same synthesis identity.

## Interpretation boundary

The synthesis is deliberately about engineering, workflow, governance and
research evidence. It must not transform software metrics into claims of
environmental impact or environmental truth.

It does not establish:

- NEMA endorsement or authorization;
- regulatory status or compliance;
- environmental truth;
- environmental impact;
- production approval;
- enforcement authority;
- emergency response authority;
- autonomous decision authority.

Real-user or field deployment still requires appropriate permission, consent,
institutional safeguards and a documented evaluation protocol.

## Award-grade value

Phase 29 provides the narrative spine for an evaluation report: each claim can
be traced to recorded evidence and its limitations rather than being presented
as an unsupported product assertion.
