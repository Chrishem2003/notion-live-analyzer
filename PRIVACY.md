﻿# Privacy Policy for Notion Live Research Analyzer

**Last Updated:** July 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
Notion Live Research Analyzer accesses data in your Notion workspace only after explicit authorization via Notion OAuth 2.0. We read metadata and records from specified databases (e.g., Project Master, Genomic Sequence Log) solely to render real-time visualizations and analytics within your Streamlit session.

## 2. How We Use Data
- Data fetched from your Notion workspace is processed transiently in memory to display dashboard metrics.
- We do not store, re-sell, or track your personal research data, sequence records, or publications on permanent external servers.

## 3. Data Storage & Security
- All authentication tokens are handled securely during your active session.
- Communication between your browser, Streamlit, and Notion APIs is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

## 4. Contact Information
For questions or data access requests regarding this integration, please contact the developer via the official repository or support channels.
