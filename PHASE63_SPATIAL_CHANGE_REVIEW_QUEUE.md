# Phase 63 — Persistent Spatial Change Review Queue\n\n## Purpose\nPersist Phase 62 review-priority outputs as immutable analytical review records so detected spatial-change candidates are not lost between sessions and can enter a human review workflow.\n\n## Implemented\n- SQLite-backed append-only spatial change review queue.\n- Input gate requires Phase 62 REVIEW_PRIORITY_ASSIGNED.\n- Validates candidate ID, source candidate SHA-256 fingerprint, priority score/tier, spatial identity, reason codes and score components.\n- Deterministic REVIEW item IDs and record fingerprints.\n- Candidate-level uniqueness prevents duplicate queue records.\n- Immutable database triggers reject UPDATE and DELETE.\n- Queue state starts only at QUEUED; no autonomous transition to reviewed/approved/rejected.\n- Persists priority, reason codes, AOI/grid identity, score components, weights and source fingerprint.\n- Read-only listing/counts and a synthetic Streamlit demonstration.\n- Non-regulatory interpretation fields remain null.\n\n## Governance boundary\nThe queue is an analytical persistence layer, not a regulatory workflow. A queued item does not establish deforestation, wetland loss, illegality, environmental truth, compliance, enforcement, emergency conditions, NEMA authorization or production approval. Human review and any governance decision remain separate.\n\n## Completion gate\nGitHub Actions must verify Phase 63 tests, compilation and Streamlit startup before Phase 63 is declared CI-green. No green status is inferred from source inspection.\n\n## Next gate\nPhase 64 — Spatial Change Human Review & Audit Binding: connect queued analytical candidates to explicit human review outcomes and append-only audit evidence without granting autonomous regulatory authority.\n
